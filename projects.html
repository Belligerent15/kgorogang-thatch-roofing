Commercial Build

Sabi Sands Exclusive Lodge

Location Mpumalanga, RSA
Duration 4 Months
Scope 1,200 sqm Main Lodge & 8 Villas

This five-star game reserve required a complete roofing solution that blended absolute luxury with traditional aesthetics. Kgorogang installed over 1,200 square meters of premium Cape Reed thatch for the main reception, dining areas, and eight private guest villas. We engineered the underlying timber frame to support massive open-air volumes, finishing with our signature Class-A fire retardant treatment.

"Piet and his team delivered beyond our wildest expectations. The roof is not just a structure; it is the breathtaking centerpiece of our lodge. The craftsmanship is world-class."

- General Manager, Sabi Sands Reserve
Residential Estate

Waterkloof Ridge Lapa & Entertainment Area

Location Pretoria, Gauteng
Duration 3 Weeks
Scope Custom 150 sqm Lapa & Bar

Designed for a high-end residential client, this project transformed an empty backyard into an elite entertainment space. We constructed a custom freestanding thatch lapa featuring exposed CCA-treated poles, intricate interior grass tying, and specialized lightning protection. The structure naturally regulates temperature, making it the perfect year-round hosting venue.

"Kgorogang operates with a level of professionalism I rarely see in construction. The site was kept immaculate, the team was polite, and the final lapa is an absolute masterpiece."

- Johan V., Private Homeowner
Restoration & Maintenance

Waterberg Eco-Resort Overhaul

Location Limpopo, RSA
Duration 6 Weeks
Scope Full Re-thatch & Fire Treatment

After severe storm damage and 15 years of weathering, this prominent eco-resort required a complete structural intervention. Kgorogang was contracted to strip the old thatch, reinforce compromised timber trusses, and apply a completely new layer of premium winter-cut grass. The project was completed ahead of schedule, minimizing resort downtime, and included a comprehensive fire retardant spray.
